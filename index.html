How the forecast works

PermitPulse learns from New York City's public record of building job filings: when each job was filed, when DOB approved it, and what kind of job it was.

1

Data

NYC DOB NOW: Build – Job Application Filings, published on NYC Open Data. About 967,000 filings since 2016.

Our database (Supabase) stores the model, a sample of 2,000 training filings, and every project saved here.

View the dataset
2

Model

Now: a linear regression trained on 263,165 filings. It uses cost, floor area, borough, work types, weekend filing and dwelling units. It is weak (R² 0.04), so its ranges are wide.

Next: gradient-boosted quantile models that predict the 10th, 50th and 90th percentile in business days. The site switches to them automatically once they are trained.

3

Validation

We train on older filings and test on newer ones, never the reverse, so the test looks like forecasting a real future permit.

We report the average error in days and how often real approvals land inside our 80% range.
