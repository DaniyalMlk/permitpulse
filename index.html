How the forecast works

PermitPulse learns from New York City's public record of building job filings: when each job was filed, when DOB approved it, and what kind of job it was.

  1. 1

    Collect the filings

    NYC DOB NOW: Build job application filings from NYC Open Data, about 967,000 filings since 2016.

    Our Supabase database stores the model, a sample of 2,000 training filings, and every project saved here.

    View the dataset
  2. 2

    Fit the model

    Now: a linear regression trained on 263,165 filings, using cost, floor area, borough, work types, weekend filing and dwelling units. It is weak (R² 0.04), so its ranges are wide.

    Next: gradient-boosted quantile models for the 10th, 50th and 90th percentile in business days. The site switches to them automatically once they are trained.

  3. 3

    Test on newer filings

    We train on older filings and test on newer ones, never the reverse, so the test looks like forecasting a real future permit.

    We report the typical error in days and how often real approvals land inside the forecast range.
